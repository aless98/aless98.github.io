High-intensity focused ultrasound can ablate tissue without an incision, but making it precise and repeatable requires solving three problems at once. FUtuRo takes on all three:

- **Robotics** — a robot positions and steers the transducer with accuracy a human hand cannot sustain.
- **Simulation** — a computational model of acoustic propagation and heat deposition predicts the ablated lesion before treatment. The model was validated in vitro and integrated into a patient-specific planning tool, published in *Scientific Reports*.
- **Augmented reality** — an AR interface gives the clinician insight into the plan and the system's state during the procedure.

The project was presented at ICRA 2024, and the surrounding work extends to AR frameworks for robot-assisted contact-rich medical tasks.
