Deep brain stimulation for Parkinson's disease depends on placing an electrode accurately within the subthalamic nucleus, a small structure that is difficult to delineate on MRI. This work combines deep-learning-based localisation of the target with automatic planning of a safe electrode trajectory that avoids critical structures on its way to the nucleus.
