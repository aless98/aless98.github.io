Validating a mixed-reality guidance tool in the operating room is difficult and slow, so a repeatable bench platform is needed first. This work designs a head phantom that reproduces the anatomy relevant to external ventricular drain placement closely enough to measure how accurately a mixed-reality guidance tool positions the catheter.
