FUtuRo brings together the three ingredients that focused ultrasound surgery needs to become precise and repeatable: a robot to position and steer the transducer, simulation to predict the ablation before it happens, and augmented reality to give the clinician insight into what the system is doing. This paper describes the project's architecture and how the components fit together.
