High-intensity focused ultrasound delivers thermal ablation non-invasively, but predicting the resulting lesion depends on modelling both acoustic propagation and heat deposition in tissue. This work develops such a model, validates its predictions against in vitro measurements, and integrates it into a planning tool that adapts to patient-specific anatomy.
