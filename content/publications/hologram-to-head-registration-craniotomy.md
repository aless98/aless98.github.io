For a mixed-reality craniotomy tool to be useful, the hologram of the surgical plan must sit on the patient's head in the right place. This paper isolates that registration step and evaluates the algorithm that aligns the virtual model to the real head, characterising the accuracy achievable before the tool is taken into a clinical setting.

This work is the foundation of the multicentric system later published in *Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine*.
