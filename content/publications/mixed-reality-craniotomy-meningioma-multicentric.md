Craniotomy planning requires transferring a pre-operative plan derived from CT/MRI onto the patient's head in the operating room. This work presents an automatic mixed-reality system that performs this transfer without external fiducial markers, aligning the virtual plan to the patient's anatomy directly on a head-mounted display so the surgeon can see the planned bone flap in situ.

The system was evaluated in a multicentric study, assessing registration accuracy and usability across several neurosurgical centres and operators.
